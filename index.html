Our mission
To combat senior isolation, bringing joy through intergenerational relationships while empowering the next generation of compassionate leaders.

Nursing homes take care of residents' medical needs, but loneliness often goes unaddressed. The Smile Exchange fills that gap with student-led companionship that makes a real difference to residents' mental and emotional well-being.

It's a true exchange. Seniors get company and practical comfort, and students learn empathy, leadership and how to run a business that does good.

20%of Hialeah's population is 65 or older, among the highest in Florida
60%of nursing-home residents report chronic isolation
What we do

Three ways we show up

Every visit combines time together, a care package and a letter afterward, so the connection lasts beyond the day.

01

Companionship visits

We spend the afternoon with residents playing dominoes, doing activities and just talking. Seniors get company, and students get to learn from them.

02

Care packages

Every resident we visit receives a branded tote of hygiene and comfort items, chosen with input from nursing-home staff.

It was so nice to hear your stories on Saturday. See you next visit!— your friends at TSE
03

Handwritten letters

After each visit, our volunteers write personal letters to the residents they met, so the smile lasts long after we leave.

Inside every care package

We picked each item with nursing-home staff and will keep improving it based on what residents tell us. The focus is comfort, hygiene and accessibility.

  • Soft throw blanket
  • Non-slip medical grip socks
  • Gentle skin moisturizer
  • Rose or lavender scented spray
  • Shaving cream and aftershave
  • Disposable shaving kit
  • Incontinence pads
  • A snack and a handwritten card

    Good to know

    Who runs The Smile Exchange?+

    Students in the MLEC Finance Class of 2027 run it as part of Junior Achievement of Greater Miami's Company Program. Our teacher, Ms. Villanueva, advises the team, and students make every decision, from choosing suppliers to keeping the books.

    Where does my donation go?+

    It goes to care-package items, the totes and printing that make them, and transportation to visit days. Any profit is reinvested in next season's visits, and we publish our full profit-and-loss statement on this page.

    How do you choose what goes in the care packages?+

    We asked nursing-home staff what residents actually need, and we update the list based on residents' feedback. Scents are gentle, and every item is chosen for comfort, hygiene or accessibility.

    How do you protect residents' privacy?+

    We cover residents' faces in every photo we share, and we never post their names.

    Can our nursing home request a visit?+

    Yes, please! Message us on Instagram at @thesmileexchange27 or use the sign-up form and choose "Nursing home or care facility."

    Will I get a receipt for my gift?+

    Yes, our checkout page emails you a receipt. If you need one for tax purposes, message us before you give and we'll tell you what we can provide.
