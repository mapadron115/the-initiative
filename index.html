Our mission
We connect the youngest and oldest people in our community who are too often left on their own, and we do it with our own hands.

Common Thread started in September 2023 when five students at Westbrook High School noticed two things in the same week: a foster agency asking for duffel bags because kids were moving homes with trash bags, and a nursing home asking for visitors because many residents had none.

We decided to help both. Today our members run monthly drives, build care kits by hand, and visit seniors every month. We publish where every dollar goes, and our faculty advisor signs off on all spending.

Show up in person

Money matters, but so does a familiar face. Every program includes time spent with the people we serve.

Stay local

Every kit, gift and visit stays within our county, through partners we know by name.

Keep open books

We share what we raise and what we spend, every semester, down to the dollar.

Something of their own

When a child enters foster care, they often arrive with only the clothes they're wearing. We work with our county's child welfare office and two foster family networks to make sure every child gets something that belongs to them.

Nobody should go a month without a visitor

Many nursing-home residents rarely get visitors. Our members spend Saturday afternoons playing games, making music, helping with video calls to family, and simply talking.

    Good to know

    Is my donation tax-deductible?+

    Donations are made through our fiscal sponsor, a registered 501(c)(3) public charity, so gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You'll receive a receipt by email.

    How do you protect the privacy of the kids you help?+

    We never meet foster children directly or collect their information. Caseworkers share anonymous wish lists using first names or ages only, and they deliver everything we pack.

    Who oversees the money?+

    Our student treasurer keeps the books, our faculty advisor approves every purchase, and we publish a full report each semester.

    Can students from other schools join?+

    Yes. We opened a chapter at Lincoln High in 2026, and we'll help any student start one at their school. Sign up above and pick "High school student".

    Can my business sponsor an event?+

    We'd love that. Sponsors can donate supplies, host a give-back night or match donations during a drive. Use the sign-up form and choose "Local business".
